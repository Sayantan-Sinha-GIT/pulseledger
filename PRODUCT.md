# PulseLedger

PulseLedger is a public status page and private monitor desk for the owner's independently operated apps. It earns trust by showing actual checks and a plain-language incident history. Its portfolio deployment monitors endpoints for Haan, StyleShopZ, CodeSense, Grepless, ArthaSetu, and Dispatch. The public experience is for visitors; the owner area is for monitoring and incident response. PRD.md is the authoritative product specification.
